About Me

I Build & Design with Purpose.

My name is AJ Lear Gillaco, and I'm on a mission to bridge the gap between design and technology. As an IT student at my second year, I've chosen to pursue both disciplines simultaneously because I believe the best digital products are ones where the engineering and the aesthetics are inseparable.

On the design side, I work with Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator to create posters, brand identities, infographics, and visual content. On the development side, I build systems using Java, Python, and web technologies with a growing passion for front-end interfaces that feel as good as they look.

I see every project whether it's a management system or a social media poster as a design problem first. My creative goal is to eventually work at the intersection of UI/UX design and front-end engineering, crafting experiences that are both functional and beautiful.

"Good design is not just what it looks like it's how it works and how it makes people feel."
